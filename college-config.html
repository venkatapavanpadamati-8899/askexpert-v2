College Escalation Policy

This application follows your college's approved escalation procedure. The system does not independently contact police or external emergency services.

Default Escalation Flow

  1. Emergency SOS → Security / Authorized Responder
  2. Level 1 → HOD / Women Safety Cell
  3. Level 2 → Principal / Designated Authority
  4. Level 3 → External Emergency Service (per college-approved protocol only)

⚠ External escalation is controlled by the college administration, not by this application.
