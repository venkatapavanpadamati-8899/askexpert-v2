## Slide 2: Problem Statement
- **The Gap in Mentorship:** Students struggle to find verified, accessible industry experts for career and technical guidance.
- **Campus Safety Challenges:** Existing college emergency systems lack real-time reporting, cross-college data isolation, and secure handling of sensitive evidence.

## Slide 3: Our Solution: AskExpert
- A unified platform serving two critical needs:
  1. **Expert Consultation:** Connect students with verified professionals.
  2. **Campus Safety:** Provide a robust, real-time emergency reporting system for college students.

## Slide 4: Key Features - Consultation
- **OTP Authentication:** Passwordless, highly secure login.
- **Expert KYC Flow:** Rigorous onboarding requiring administrative approval to ensure platform integrity.
- **Real-Time Chat:** Instant communication backed by secure databases.
- **Wallet & Payments:** Integrated, secure payment system with replay protection.

## Slide 5: Key Features - Campus Safety Module
- **Instant SOS:** One-tap emergency reporting with GPS location.
- **Anonymous Reporting:** Empowers students to report issues safely.
- **Evidence Management:** Secure upload and storage of photos/videos.
- **Cross-College Isolation:** Ensuring data privacy—authorities can only access incidents from their respective institutions.

## Slide 6: System Architecture & Technologies
- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript (Fast, lightweight).
- **Backend & DB:** Supabase (PostgreSQL), Edge Functions.
- **Security:** Strict Row Level Security (RLS) to prevent unauthorized access.
- **Deployment:** Hosted on Vercel for global edge availability.

## Slide 7: Security & Quality Assurance
- **Robust Testing:** Automated regression testing using **Playwright**.
- **Test Results:** 41/41 tests passing in the live production environment.
- **Security Validation:** Tested against role escalation, duplicate spam, and data leakage. 
- **Production URL:** `askexpert-v2.vercel.app`

## Slide 8: Future Enhancements
- AI-driven chat moderation and automated response suggestions.
- Video consultation integration (WebRTC).
- Expansion of the safety module to local law enforcement.

## Slide 9: Conclusion
- **AskExpert** successfully merges commercial utility (mentorship) with social responsibility (campus safety).
- Fully deployed, tested, and secured for real-world usage.
